## The expert's suggestions
- **Lower the indoor temperature by 1 degree: saves 566 kWh a year** (about 4 % of the heating), at no cost. At 2.2 kr/kWh that is about 1,250 kr a year.
- **Low-flow shower heads: saves 110 kWh a year** (cost 1.19 kr per saved kWh).
- Nothing on insulation, windows, ventilation or the heating system.

## What it tells us
- The best anchor for the heat pump in 2025 is **15,109 kWh** (13,599 heating plus 1,510 hot water), with the previous owner's habits.
- 2025's heating, at 13,599 kWh over 3,285 degree-days, is about 4.1 kWh per degree-day. What we measure now is about 1.5–1.75 kWh per degree-day in a mild autumn. That is still a factor 2–3 apart, so either the heating gets much dearer when it is cold (the elpatron, poorer efficiency) or the previous owner's heat pump behaved differently (see `heating-system.md`, the elpatron section).
- Atemp 151 m² of which 68 m² is "Bia": the basement is a large part of the heated floor area, so the basement radiators (four old ones with old valves) matter more for the heating than the first guess of 15–35 % of the heat.
- The house has **only natural ventilation**: the CO2 above 1000 ppm at night in Astrid's room and the damp basement (a dehumidifier running all the time) fit that.
- **Radon has never been measured officially**, while the Airthings shows 200–370 Bq/m³ at the living level and a very high long-term value. Get it measured properly.
